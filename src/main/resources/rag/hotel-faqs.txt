HOTEL FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - THE GRAND MERIDIAN

Q: What are the check-in and check-out times?
A: Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability.

Q: Is there a fee for late checkout?
A: Late checkout until 2:00 PM is available for $75. Platinum and Gold loyalty members receive complimentary late checkout.

Q: What is the cancellation policy?
A: Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged one night's rate.

Q: Is parking available?
A: Yes, we offer valet parking at $65/night and self-parking at $45/night. Electric vehicle charging stations are available.

Q: Do you have a business center?
A: Yes, our 24-hour business center is located on the 2nd floor with printing, scanning, and private meeting rooms.

Q: What is the WiFi password?
A: Complimentary high-speed WiFi is available throughout the hotel. Connect to "GrandMeridian_Guest" and enter your room number and last name.

Q: Is there an airport shuttle?
A: We offer luxury car service to JFK, LaGuardia, and Newark airports. Rates start at $95. Please book 24 hours in advance.

Q: What are the pool hours?
A: The rooftop infinity pool is open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily. Heated pool available year-round.

Q: Do you allow pets?
A: Yes, we welcome pets under 25 lbs with a $150 pet fee per stay. Pet amenities including beds and bowls are provided.

Q: Is there a gym/fitness center?
A: Our state-of-the-art fitness center is open 24/7 on the 3rd floor. Personal trainers available by appointment.

Q: What floor is the restaurant on?
A: The Grand Brasserie is on the lobby level, Sky Lounge is on the 30th floor rooftop, and Sakura Japanese restaurant is on the 5th floor.

Q: Do you have room service?
A: 24-hour in-room dining is available. Our full menu is accessible via the in-room tablet or by calling extension 1.

Q: What is the dress code for restaurants?
A: Smart casual for The Grand Brasserie, business casual for Sky Lounge. No athletic wear or flip-flops in dining areas after 6 PM.

Q: Can I request extra towels or pillows?
A: Absolutely! Contact our concierge or housekeeping at any time. Typical delivery within 15 minutes.

Q: Is there a concierge service?
A: Our AI concierge is available 24/7 via chat. Human concierge staff are available at the lobby desk from 7 AM to 11 PM.
