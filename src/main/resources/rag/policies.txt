HOTEL POLICIES - THE GRAND MERIDIAN

CHECK-IN/CHECK-OUT:
- Check-in: 3:00 PM | Check-out: 11:00 AM
- Early check-in (from 12:00 PM): Subject to availability, $50 fee
- Late check-out (until 2:00 PM): $75 fee, complimentary for Platinum/Gold members
- Late check-out (until 4:00 PM): $150 fee, subject to availability
- Express check-out available via in-room TV or mobile app

CANCELLATION POLICY:
- Free cancellation: 48+ hours before check-in
- Within 48 hours: One night's rate charged
- No-show: Full stay charged
- Group bookings: 7 days advance notice required

PAYMENT:
- Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, JCB
- Authorization hold: One night's rate + $100 incidentals
- Currency: USD (international cards accepted)
- Invoicing available for corporate accounts

SMOKING POLICY:
- 100% smoke-free property (including e-cigarettes)
- Designated outdoor smoking area on 2nd floor terrace
- $500 cleaning fee for smoking in rooms

PET POLICY:
- Dogs and cats under 25 lbs welcome
- $150 per stay pet fee
- Maximum 2 pets per room
- Pets must be leashed in public areas
- Pet relief area on 2nd floor garden
- Pet-sitting services available

NOISE POLICY:
- Quiet hours: 10:00 PM - 8:00 AM
- Music/TV volume to be kept at reasonable levels
- Pool area closes at 10:00 PM

POOL & SPA:
- Pool hours: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Children under 12 must be accompanied by adult
- No glass containers at pool
- Spa appointments: 24-hour cancellation policy
- Spa minimum age: 16 years

SAFETY & SECURITY:
- 24-hour security personnel
- CCTV in all public areas
- Electronic key card access
- In-room safe provided
- Fire safety: Sprinklers and smoke detectors in all rooms

LOYALTY PROGRAM TIERS:
- Silver: 3+ stays/year - 10% dining discount, late checkout request priority
- Gold: 6+ stays/year - 15% dining discount, complimentary late checkout, room upgrade when available
- Platinum: 12+ stays/year - 20% dining discount, complimentary late checkout, guaranteed upgrade, welcome amenity, airport transfer

DAMAGE POLICY:
- Guests responsible for damage to room or hotel property
- Charges applied to card on file
- Incident report filed for insurance purposes

LOST & FOUND:
- Items held for 90 days
- Contact front desk or email lost@grandmeridian.com
- Shipping available at guest's expense
